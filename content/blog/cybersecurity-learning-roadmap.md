My roadmap is built around technical depth rather than checklist completion. I want each stage of learning to produce either a project, a writeup, or a repeatable lab workflow.

## Current focus areas

- Packet analysis and protocol tracing
- Network scanning mechanics and detection signals
- Linux telemetry and system behavior
- Foundations of cryptographic protocols

## Medium-term direction

I am moving toward graduate-level work in cybersecurity, with emphasis on network security, digital forensics, and research communication. That means spending more time on careful technical writing, methodology, and evidence-based reasoning.
